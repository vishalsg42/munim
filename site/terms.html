What Munim is

A command line tool and MCP server that holds a separate session with each of your clients' accounts, so a coding agent can read across all of them and make changes inside one you have named. It runs locally. There is no service to sign up for and no account to create.

Your responsibilities

  • Authorisation. Only connect accounts you are entitled to act on. If they belong to a client, make sure your arrangement with that client permits it.
  • Provider terms. Using Munim to reach a service does not change your agreement with that service. Google, Cloudflare, Vercel, Supabase and the rest each have their own terms, and they still apply.
  • Your credentials. They are stored in your operating machine, in a file only your user account can read, and are yours to protect, as is the machine they are on.
  • Changes you approve. Munim can alter DNS records and other live configuration. Where a change would replace something a person put there deliberately, it asks first. Approving it is your decision and its consequences are yours.
